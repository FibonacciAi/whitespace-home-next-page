Privacy

Private by architecture.

This notice describes the current Whitespace for Mac and iPhone companion. It will be reviewed again against the final production build before App Store submission.

Where your context lives

Your canonical Context Graph, application data, uploads, and named credentials remain on the Mac or in services you explicitly connect. Provider and connector credentials are held in macOS Keychain rather than copied into the iPhone app.

The iPhone companion

The companion connects to a paired Mac using a revocable token stored in iOS Keychain. It may keep connection state, cached signals, saved items, and interrupted Drops on the device. User-selected notes, links, photos, and files are sent to the paired Mac when you use Drop.

Camera, microphone, and network

  • Camera access is used only to scan a short-lived pairing QR.
  • Microphone access begins only when you start live voice or another recording feature.
  • Local-network access lets the companion reach the paired Mac.

Models and connected services

When you explicitly choose model synthesis or live voice, the content needed for that request may be sent to the selected provider under that provider’s terms. Live Voice uses a short-lived session; the long-lived provider key stays on the Mac. Connected sources process data under their own policies.

Tracking and advertising

The current applications do not include advertising or cross-app tracking behavior. If diagnostics or analytics are added, this notice and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before release.

Questions

For privacy questions or deletion help, contact the Whitespace team. Do not include credentials or private graph content in email.
