Whitespace support

We’ll help you get reconnected.

Support for Whitespace for Mac and the native iPhone companion—pairing, private connectivity, builds, and product feedback.

Contact

Email the Whitespace team with the app version, device model, and a short description of what happened. Never send API keys, pairing tokens, QR codes, or private graph content.

hi@whitespace.dev ↗

Pairing the companion

On the Mac, open Settings → iPhone Companion, activate the bridge, and show the short-lived pairing QR. On iPhone, open Settings and scan that QR. Both devices must be able to reach the same private network route.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm Whitespace is open on the paired Mac.
  • Check the connection state in companion Settings.
  • Retry a queued Drop instead of recreating it.
  • If the Mac address changed, use the address-repair control without replacing the Keychain token.
